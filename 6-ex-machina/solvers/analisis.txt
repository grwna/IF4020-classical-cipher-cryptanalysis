Enigma workings:
For encryption
Keyboards: plaintext input
Lampboard: ciphertext output

For decryption, it is the exact opposite, you input ciphetext, plaintext lights up


The files:
- plugboard
- reflector
- rotor
- machine
- enigma

Plugboards are plugs on the machine 
A reflector is the output of electricity after flowing through all rotors, it reflects the signal back as a different through the rotors. During this reflection, a letter is substituted into another.
Overall the amount of letter changes are: 
    - 2m + 1 -> no plugboard
    - With a plugboard, a letter is substituted again into another letter. Example: A -> J, it means if the rotor's final output is A, it turns to J, and vice versa (mirrored).
        - NOTE: plugboard subsitution happens before and after the rotors 

When the rotors rotates, the electricity path changes. Each rotor usees a pawl to move it, and the previous rotor has a notch. Only the first rotor moves every time, the second only moves when the first rotor is at its notch.

Key/configuration:
- Rotor order: which rotor to which slot (8 rotors, pick 3, the order can be switched up)
- Ring settings <-> includes notch: offset between the ratchet and the alphabet ring (where the notch lives)
- Rotor starting positions: starting key for each rotor
- Plugboard connections

Dari KODE:
- Ada 8 Rotor berbeda, yang masing-masing memiliki order dan ring setting yang berbeda (ini sama dengan standard naval). 
- Hanya reflector B
- Ring settings hanya memengaruhi wiring dari rotor, dan tidak memengaruhi notch.
- Plugboard harus 10 pasangan, atau kosong.
- Everything else exactly the same, each configuration juga sama persis.

Overall konfigurasi pada soal ini lebih simpel dari standard Enigma M3.
